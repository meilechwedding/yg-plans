Service

Site plans.

Lot layouts, setbacks, drainage, driveways. The work that gets you through planning board — and tells the builder where the foundation goes.

01 — Deliverables

What we draw for site plans.

01 Lot survey review

We read the survey and confirm the lot lines, easements, and existing conditions before drawing.

02 Zoning compliance

Setbacks, lot coverage, FAR, height — every dimension checked against the code.

03 Setbacks & easements

Drawn precisely. Where you can build, where you can't, and why.

04 Drainage planning

Where the water goes in a storm. Drywells, swales, grading — coordinated with civil.

05 Site grading

Cut and fill, finished floor elevations, retaining walls. The foundation's set in the dirt before it's in concrete.

06 Planning board package

Full submission set. We attend the meeting and answer the board's questions.
