Service

Semi-attached.

Side-by-side residences that read as one composition. Party walls, light, privacy, and circulation worked out on paper first.

01 — Deliverables

What we draw for semi-attached.

01 Party wall studies

Sound, fire, and code. The shared wall worked out so neither household hears the other.

02 Unit layouts

Mirror or independent. Each side a complete home, not a half-house.

03 Privacy & light analysis

Window placement, side-yard offsets, and screening so each unit feels its own.

04 Egress

Two paths out of every unit, drawn to code, easy to live with.

05 Elevations

Two driveways, two front doors, one composition that reads as one building.

06 Permit set

Stamped and code-checked. Filed and shepherded through approval.
