Service

Custom homes.

Single-family residences drawn around your lot, your zoning, your family. From first layout to permit-ready set.

01 — Deliverables

What we draw for custom homes.

01 Site analysis

Topography, zoning, setbacks, and orientation. We figure out what your lot actually supports before drawing anything.

02 Layouts & circulation

Room placement, sight lines, daily flow. How a family actually moves through a house.

03 Elevations

Massing, materials, proportions, and the small details that make a house look quietly right from the curb.

04 Construction documents

Full drawing set the builder can build from — plans, sections, schedules, details.

05 Permit set

Stamped and code-checked, ready for the building department. We handle revisions.

06 Builder coordination

On call through framing. Field changes drawn cleanly so the foundation matches the plan.
