03 / Studio

Drawn in Monsey.

Yidel Grunberger and Tuli Strasser run YG Plan from Monsey, drawing homes, additions, shuls, and multifamily projects for builders, developers, and mosdos across Rockland County.

01.
Listen.

A conversation first. The lot, the zoning, the budget, the goals — what you’re trying to build, and what can’t be compromised.

02.
Draft.

First plans within two weeks. Layouts, elevations, the shape of the thing on paper — something real to react to.

03.
Refine.

Move a wall. Add a window. Cut a closet. We revise until the plan matches the way the space will actually be used.

04.
Permit.

Drawings prepared for filing. Revisions handled when the building department or planning board has notes.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get plans?

First drafts usually within two to three weeks of our first conversation. Most projects go through two to three revision rounds before the permit set is ready — typically six to ten weeks from start to filing, depending on scope.

Are you licensed architects?

YG Plan is an architectural design studio. We draw plans, layouts, elevations, and construction documents. When a project requires a licensed stamp, we coordinate with a licensed New York architect or engineer.

Do you handle permits and planning board?

We prepare the full drawing package and work with your filer or engineer on revisions through the building department and Planning Board.

Do you work outside Monsey?

Yes. Mostly across Rockland County — Monsey, Airmont, New City, Spring Valley, Suffern, Pomona, New Hempstead, Chestnut Ridge. We’ve done projects in northern Bergen County, NJ as well.

I just have an idea. Is that enough to start?

That’s where most projects begin. A rough sketch. A Pinterest board. Even just “I want my parents to have their own apartment downstairs.” We’ll take it from there. The first conversation is free.
