Service

Multifamily.

Walk-ups, mid-rise, mixed residential. Unit mix, stack alignment, egress, zoning yield — sorted early so the math works.

01 — Deliverables

What we draw for multifamily.

01 Zoning yield study

How many units the lot supports under current zoning. The number that decides whether the project pencils.

02 Unit mix planning

Bed/bath mix tuned to the market and the building. Studios, 2s, 3s — what the rent roll actually wants.

03 Stack alignment

Plumbing, structure, and mechanicals stacked floor-to-floor so the building is buildable.

04 Egress & code

Two stairs or one, sprinklered or not, corridor widths — code resolved before drawing the units.

05 Elevations & sections

Massing, materials, parapets, balconies. The building seen from the street and in cut.

06 Full permit set

Stamped and filed. We coordinate with structural, MEP, and civil through approval.
