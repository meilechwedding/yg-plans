Service

Shuls & mosdos.

Buildings for community life — sanctuaries, classrooms, social halls. Sightlines, acoustics, separate flows, and growth phasing built in from the start.

01 — Deliverables

What we draw for shuls & mosdos.

01 Program & flow studies

Who comes when. Weekday vs. Shabbos, men vs. women, daily vs. yom tov.

02 Sanctuary layout

Seating, aron, bimah, sight lines. Drawn so every seat sees the davening.

03 Mechanical coordination

HVAC, electrical, sprinklers — coordinated above the ceiling so the sanctuary stays clean.

04 Acoustic considerations

Ceiling shape, finishes, and absorption tuned so the chazzan carries without amplification.

05 Phased growth planning

Today's building, ready for tomorrow's wing. Foundations and utilities sized for the next phase.

06 Full permit set

Stamped and filed. We work with the gabbai and the building committee through approval.

03 — Process

How we work.

01
Listen.

Meet with the gabbai and building committee. Understand the program before we draw a wall.

02
Draft.

Sanctuary layout and program diagram in two weeks. Reviewed by the full committee.

03
Refine.

Tune for sightlines, mechanical, and growth phases. Iterate until the committee approves.

04
Permit.

Stamped set filed. We support the committee through planning board and building department.
