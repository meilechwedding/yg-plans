Service

Renovations.

New living room. Dormer up top. Full gut. We work with the bones you have and the budget you have, and keep it moving.

01 — Deliverables

What we draw for renovations.

01 As-built survey

Field-measured existing conditions. Real walls, real floors — not the original drawings.

02 Scope studies

Two or three options for what could change. Cost-aware, not just wish-list.

03 Layouts

New plans drawn against existing conditions. We keep what works and rework what doesn't.

04 Structural coordination

Beam sizes, headers, footings for additions — coordinated with a structural engineer.

05 Permit set

Stamped and filed. We handle the inspector and the field changes.

06 Builder coordination

On call through framing. Existing conditions surprises drawn cleanly as they come up.
